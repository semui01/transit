      About this planner

      Journey times are estimates from track distance plus typical stop and transfer times. Allow a few extra minutes to wait for your first train. Fares are adult card fares; student, senior and concession fares are lower.

      Keeping it up to date

      • Stations with an announced opening date switch on by themselves on that date. Bedok South, Sungei Bedok and Xilin are already loaded for 26 Dec 2026, and so is the fare change on the same day.
      • A weekly check compares this planner with the LTA rail network page and its system map, and flags new stations or a new map version so the data can be refreshed.
